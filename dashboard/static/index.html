About OceanEmbed

Smart India Hackathon 2026 • Problem Statement 26066

Executive Overview

OceanEmbed is a deep learning framework designed to reconstruct subsurface ocean temperature profiles down to 1000 meters strictly from surface satellite observations (SST, SSS, SLA, ocean currents, and surface winds). By learning a 64-dimensional latent ocean embedding over localized 5×5 spatial patches, OceanEmbed captures nonlinear upper-ocean physics and thermocline displacements without requiring computationally heavy numerical ocean models.
